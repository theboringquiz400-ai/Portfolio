WHAT WE BUILD

Custom apps shaped around your workflow

No generic templates or forced processes. Here are the types of custom apps we build most often.

Internal operations & dashboards

One central platform to run and monitor your day-to-day operations.

  • Custom fleet, vehicle and equipment management
  • Live booking tracking and team dispatching
  • Real-time project cost and profit/loss calculation
  • Role-based access so staff only see what they need

Client portals & booking systems

Secure self-service portals that give your customers full visibility.

  • Client accounts with secure self-service logins
  • Online appointment and reservation scheduling
  • Real-time project status and milestone tracking
  • Digital document sharing, quotes and sign-offs

Inventory & billing systems

Replace paper logbooks and broken spreadsheets with clean software.

  • Accurate stock counts with low-inventory alerts
  • Instant invoice generation and receipt printing
  • Monthly sales, purchases and expense summaries
  • Faster audits with zero paper trails lost

Group finance & custom tools

Purpose-built software for specific workflows off-the-shelf tools can't handle.

  • Group savings, deposits and contribution tracking
  • Rule engines built for how your group actually runs
  • Responsive web interface that works on any device
  • Automated reconciliation and exportable audit logs

Custom app FAQs

How much does a custom app cost?

It depends on what you need. After a free discovery call we send a written proposal with scope and cost.

How long does it take to build?

It depends on the size of the app. We give you an estimate in the proposal, and you can use an early version while we keep building.

What if we already have a system that almost works?

We'd rather improve what you have than rebuild it. The discovery call helps us work out which is better for you.

Got a process held together by spreadsheets?

That's usually the perfect custom app. Book a free discovery call and we'll map it, whether or not you hire us.
