Terms and Payment Policy

Plain language on purpose. This page is a summary. For every project, the written proposal and signed agreement we share with you are what apply. If they differ from this page, the signed agreement wins.

In short

  • We scope the work in writing before we start.
  • You pay an advance, then in milestones as work is delivered.
  • You own the code once the project is fully paid for.
  • Refunds depend on how much work has been delivered. Details below.

We try to keep it accurate, but it isn't an offer of a fixed price or timeline. Those come only in your written proposal. Links to other sites are for convenience, and we aren't responsible for their content.

2. How projects work

  • Discovery call: free, with no obligation.
  • Proposal: we write down what we'll build, how long it should take and what it costs.
  • Agreement: work starts once you accept the proposal and sign our service agreement, and the advance is paid.
  • Changes: anything outside the agreed scope is quoted separately, so there are no surprises.
  • Your part: timely feedback, content and approvals. If these are delayed, the timeline moves too.

3. Payments

  • An advance of [X]% is due before work starts. The rest is paid in milestones set out in the proposal.
  • Prices are in [INR] and exclude GST and any third-party costs unless the proposal says otherwise.
  • Invoices are due within [7] days. We may pause work on overdue invoices, and we'll tell you before we do.
  • Final files and handover happen after the last milestone is paid.

4. Refunds and cancellation

Custom software involves real work from day one, so refunds depend on how far the project has gone:

  • Before work starts: the advance is refunded in full, less any payment-gateway or third-party costs already incurred.
  • After work starts: the advance covers work already done. If you cancel, we'll invoice only for the work delivered up to that point and refund any balance left over.
  • Delivered and approved milestones: these aren't refundable.
  • Milestones not yet delivered: if you cancel, we'll refund or credit what you paid for them, after deducting the work in progress.
  • If we can't deliver: if we miss an agreed milestone by more than [X] days for reasons within our control, you can cancel and get a refund for undelivered work.
  • Third-party costs (domains, hosting, licences, API credits) already paid on your behalf aren't refundable.

To request a refund, email [EMAIL] with your project name and reason. We'll reply within [7] days and, where a refund is due, pay it within [14] days.

5. Ownership of the work

  • Once the project is fully paid for, you own the custom code and content we build for you.
  • We keep ownership of our general tools, templates and know-how, and give you a licence to use them as part of your project.
  • Open-source and third-party components keep their own licences.
  • Client names and project details are confidential. We only show your work in our portfolio with your permission.

6. Warranty and support

After handover we fix bugs in the agreed scope for [30] days at no extra cost. New features, changes to scope, and ongoing support or hosting are quoted separately. We'll never promise "no bugs", but we'll fix what's ours.

7. Third-party services and AI

Projects may rely on services from others, such as hosting, payment gateways, messaging platforms and AI providers. Their prices, terms and availability are outside our control. AI output can be wrong, so important decisions need a human check. We'll tell you which services your project uses before we start.

8. Limits of liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability for any claim connected to a project is limited to the fees you paid us for that project. We aren't liable for indirect losses such as lost profit or lost data, except where the law doesn't allow us to exclude them.

9. Governing law and disputes

These terms are governed by the laws of India. If a problem comes up, please contact us first so we can try to sort it out in good faith. Unresolved disputes go to the courts of [CITY], India, or to the process in your signed agreement.

10. Changes

We may update this page. Changes don't affect a project you've already signed up for. Privacy details are in our privacy policy.
