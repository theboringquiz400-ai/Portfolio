The four steps

From first call to software that fits

Discovery call

Free. You explain the problem, we ask about your tools and workflow. You leave with clarity, even if we never work together.

Proposal

A written proposal in plain English: what we'll build, what's included, the cost and the timeline. If building isn't worth it for you, we'll say so.

Early-access build

You get early access to a working version. Use it, tell us what's missing or wrong, and we build features around your feedback.

Handover & support

Documentation and a walkthrough for your team. Ongoing support afterwards if you want it, never forced.

Step 1: we understand the problem
Step 2: you try the first version
Step 3: we improve it from your feedback

Good to know

Process FAQs

What do you need from us during a project?

Someone who can explain how your business works and give feedback on early versions. That's the most important input.

What if the scope changes?

Tell us. We'll discuss what changes, what it affects and any change in cost, and agree it before starting that work.

Can you support the app after launch?

Yes, if you want it. Support is optional and agreed separately.
