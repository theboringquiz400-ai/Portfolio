THREE AGENTS, THREE FLAVOURS

Each with its own job, tools and guardrails

Start with one agent, add others when you're ready. Every step is logged and reviewable.

Support agent

Answers the routine, escalates the rest

Trained on your docs, order data and policies to resolve common customer requests.

  • Answers customer questions kindly, day and night
  • Looks up order statuses and policy rules in real time
  • Hands tricky cases to a human with complete conversation history
  • Logs every action so your team can audit every reply
Sales agent

Follows up so your team can close

Replies to new leads quickly, keeping prospects warm and qualifying interest.

  • Replies to inbound leads within minutes before they go cold
  • Asks qualifying questions based on budget, scope and timeline
  • Finds available times and books discovery calls on your calendar
  • Logs all lead details and message histories to your CRM
Ops agent

The teammate who never copy-pastes

Handles repetitive back-office data chores and reconciles your tools.

  • Matches invoices and receipts against bank transactions
  • Chases missing customer documents and form submissions
  • Flags billing or data discrepancies before they cause issues
  • Delivers weekly summaries of handled vs flagged tasks

TRANSPARENCY FIRST

Every step logged. Nothing silent.

Black-box AI is how companies end up in trouble. Our agents show their work: which data they read, which tool they used and why they answered the way they did. Your team reviews the conversations and can change any rule.

  • Transcript and action log per conversation
  • Confidence thresholds with automatic human hand-off
  • Rules you control: approved wording, limits, banned claims
  • We don't use your data to train models
✓Reads: your help docs and records

AI agent FAQs

How much does an AI agent cost?

It depends on what the agent does and which tools it connects to. We also discuss ongoing AI usage and hosting costs in the proposal, so there are no surprises.

Will it say something wrong and embarrass us?

Guardrails are the point: confidence thresholds, approved wording, banned claims and automatic hand-off to a person. The agent only acts alone inside rules you've agreed.

Can it replace my team?

No, and we'd tell you if you asked us to try. Agents take over the repetitive work so your people can focus on work that needs judgement.
