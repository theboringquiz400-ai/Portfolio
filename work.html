AI App

TourCRM

A CRM for travel and tour businesses. When a customer needs a plan fast, the AI drafts an itinerary and the team can edit it as needed. It also tracks sales targets and who is working on what, and uses attribute-based access control (ABAC) so each person only sees what they're allowed to.
