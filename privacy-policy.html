Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]. Written in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear, contact us at [EMAIL] and we'll explain it.

1. Who we are

Solvecraft ("we", "us") is a software studio that builds custom apps, websites, AI agents and automations. This policy covers this website and the services we deliver. Contact for privacy questions: [EMAIL]. Business details: [LEGAL NAME, CITY, INDIA].

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us: your name, email, company, and whatever you tell us about your project. We use this to reply and, if you become a client, to deliver the work.
  • When you browse this site: we don't currently use analytics. If we add analytics later, we'll update this policy. We don't use advertising trackers and we don't sell data.
  • Cookies: this site does not set tracking cookies.

3. Project data and AI

When we build apps, AI agents or automations for you, we process only the data you give us for that purpose, such as documents, records and policies. We:

  • Do not use your data to train public AI models.
  • Limit access to the team members working on your project.
  • Delete our working copies at handover unless you ask us to keep supporting you.
  • Will sign an NDA or data-processing agreement on request.

If your project uses third-party AI services, we choose the provider based on your needs and budget and tell you which one before we start. Data sent to that provider is only what the feature needs, and we pick settings that stop it being used to train models wherever the provider allows.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. We share it only with providers we need to run our work (email, hosting, project tools, AI services as above), or if the law requires it.

5. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us what personal data we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, and raise a grievance. Email [EMAIL] and we'll respond within 30 days. If you are outside India, you may have similar rights under your local law and we'll honour them where they apply.

6. Retention

Enquiry data is deleted if we don't work together within 12 months. Client project data is kept while we work together and for any period required by law or accounting, then deleted or returned to you.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you promptly.

8. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll email active clients.
