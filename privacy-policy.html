Privacy Policy

Written in plain language on purpose. If anything is unclear, contact us at Gurushivag@gmail.com and we'll explain it.

In short

  • We only collect what you send us when you get in touch.
  • No ads, no tracking cookies, and we never sell your data.
  • Your project data stays yours and is not used to train public AI models.
  • You can ask to see, fix or delete your data at any time.

1. Who we are

mtconsultancies ("we", "us") is a software studio that builds custom apps, websites, AI agents and automations. This policy covers this website and the services we deliver. Business details: [LEGAL NAME], [CITY], India. Privacy contact: Gurushivag@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • When you contact us by WhatsApp or email: your name, phone number or email address, company, and whatever you tell us about your project. We use this to reply and, if you become a client, to deliver the work.
  • When you browse this site: we don't use analytics, advertising trackers or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) and Google Fonts may receive technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the page. We don't see or use that data.

If we add analytics or any cookies later, we'll update this policy first.

3. Project data and AI

When we build apps, AI agents or automations for you, we process only the data you give us for that purpose, such as documents, records and policies. We:

  • Do not use your data to train public AI models.
  • Limit access to the team members working on your project.
  • Delete our working copies at handover, unless you ask us to keep supporting you.
  • Will sign an NDA or data-processing agreement on request.

If your project uses third-party AI services, we choose the provider based on your needs and budget and tell you which one before we start. Data sent to that provider is only what the feature needs. Some providers process data outside India, and we'll tell you if that applies. We choose settings that stop your data being used to train models, wherever the provider allows.

4. Sharing

We don't sell or rent your data. We share it only with providers we need to run our work (messaging and email, hosting, project tools, AI services as above), or if the law requires it. Some of these providers may store data outside India.

5. Your rights

You can ask us what personal data we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, withdraw your consent, and raise a complaint. Email Gurushivag@gmail.com and we'll respond within 30 days.

Grievance contact: [NAME / ROLE], Gurushivag@gmail.com.

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 is being phased in, and we follow its principles already. If you are outside India, you may have similar rights under your local law and we'll honour them where they apply.

6. Retention

  • Enquiries: deleted if we don't work together within 12 months.
  • Client project data: data that belongs to you is deleted or returned to you at handover.
  • Our business records (such as contracts and invoices) are kept for as long as the law or accounting rules require, then deleted.

7. Security

We use encrypted connections and restrict access to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we'll tell you without undue delay.

8. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll email active clients.

Questions about your data?

Message us and we'll answer in plain words.
