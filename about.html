WHY WE EXIST

Software should fit the problem

mtconsultancies is a small team of developers building custom software for small businesses. Too often, small teams are sold big platforms for problems a simpler tool could fix.

So we work the other way around. Understand the problem. Build the smallest thing that solves it. Let you try it early and shape it with us.

  • A small team, so you talk to the people doing the work
  • We tell you when something isn't worth building
  • Practical by default: simple tools first, custom code where it matters
