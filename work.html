AI App

TourCRM

The problem
Travel and tour businesses need to turn around a plan for a customer fast, and keep track of the team's work.
What we built
A CRM where AI drafts an itinerary and the team can edit it. It tracks sales targets and who is working on what, and uses attribute-based access control (ABAC) so each person only sees what they're allowed to.

  • Sales targets
  • Team tracking
  • ABAC security
